Pricing

Tell us what you're building.
We'll find the right production level.

Standard editing starts around $50 a video. Larger and more complex projects are scoped to what they actually involve — so you get a real number, not a guess.

Standard

Straightforward, high-volume editing.

Starting around $50/ video
  • Professional cuts and pacing
  • Basic colour correction and audio cleanup
  • Captions, music and basic SFX
  • Basic B-roll and text graphics
  • 1 revision round per video

Not included: complex motion graphics, advanced animation, heavy VFX, 3D, scriptwriting, full content strategy.

Advanced

More involved editing and creative treatment.

Scoped per project Quoted
  • Everything in Standard
  • Advanced storytelling and structure
  • Developed motion graphics and advanced captions
  • Detailed sound design and B-roll treatment
  • Visual effects and sophisticated transitions
  • Dedicated creative and editorial handling
  • 1 revision round per video

Scope: anything outside the agreed brief is quoted separately before we start.

Premium

Advanced creative production and campaigns.

Projects from $250+
  • Advanced motion design
  • 2D and 3D animation
  • VFX and complex compositing
  • High-end commercial and campaign editing
  • Creative direction and custom visual systems
  • Multiple deliverables

Any project from $250 requires an online consultation before confirmation — scope varies too much to quote blind.

Pricing is driven by what a project actually involves: complexity, duration, number of deliverables, animation, VFX, 3D, motion graphics, timeline, revision requirements and creative input.

That's why the $50 figure is a starting point rather than a flat rate — it keeps the door open for smaller jobs without forcing a fixed price onto work that needs more.
