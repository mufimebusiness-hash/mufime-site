FAQ

Questions, Answered

What's the difference between Simple and Complex?

Simple covers straightforward, single-source edits with one export format and minimal graphics. Complex includes everything in Simple plus motion graphics, multi-format resizing, advanced color, and heavier storytelling.

How many revision rounds are included?

Simple includes 2 rounds. Complex includes 2–3 rounds. Premium revision rounds are scoped per project during your consultation.

What's excluded from the Simple plan?

Motion graphics, multi-format resizing, custom animation, and advanced color grading are not included in Simple — those live in Complex and Premium.

Do I need to send raw, unedited footage?

Yes — raw footage (or your existing project files) gives us the most flexibility to edit, grade, and adjust pacing without quality loss.

Can I get multiple formats or aspect ratios from one edit?

Multi-format resizing (16:9, 9:16, 1:1) is included from the Complex tier upward. Simple delivers one primary format.

Do I get the source project files?

Source/project files can be included as part of a scoped agreement — let us know during your call if you need them.

What's the typical turnaround time?

Simple runs on a standard turnaround, Complex is priority, and Premium turnaround is scoped per project based on complexity.

Is rush delivery available?

Rush delivery can sometimes be arranged depending on current workload — ask about it on your Book a Call appointment.

Do you edit long-form YouTube content?

Yes — long-form YouTube is one of our core services, with narrative structure and pacing built for full-length retention.

Do you help with content strategy or pre-production?

Yes — our Process includes a dedicated Content Strategy and Pre-Production stage before any editing begins.

What happens if a project goes over the agreed scope?

We flag it before doing the extra work, and agree on additional scope and cost together — nothing gets billed as a surprise.

Do you offer unlimited revisions or unlimited videos?

No — we don't promise unlimited revisions, unlimited videos, unlimited motion graphics/VFX, or unlimited adaptations on any plan. Scope is agreed upfront so quality stays consistent.

Is there a subscription or retainer option?

Ongoing monthly work can be arranged as a retainer built from Simple/Complex/Premium pricing — ask about it on your call.

What if I'm not sure which plan fits my project?

That's exactly what the Book a Call step is for — tell us what you're trying to create and we'll recommend the right tier before any price is confirmed.

How do I get started?

Book a call using the button on this page, or send a message to mufime.business@gmail.com — we'll study your project before quoting anything.
