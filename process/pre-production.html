Inside the stage

What actually happens

01

Brief and objectives

We start with what the video is for, who it's for, and what it has to achieve. Nothing gets cut until that's settled.

02

References and direction

You send what you like the feel of. We translate that into a treatment the editor can actually work to.

03

Footage review

We go through the material before starting, so problems surface early rather than at first-cut stage.

04

Structure and outline

Longer pieces get a paper edit first: the shape of the story before the shape of the timeline.
