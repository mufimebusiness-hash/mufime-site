FAQ

Before you send anything over

Do I need to book a call?

No. Smaller projects can be submitted through the quote form and we'll reply by email.

When do I need a call?

Projects starting at $250 or more require an online consultation before confirmation.

How do I get a quote?

Submit your project details and Mufime will review them and respond by email from mufime.business@gmail.com.

How many revisions are included?

Standard and Advanced packages include one revision round per video.

What is a revision round?

One consolidated set of client feedback submitted after the first draft. Major creative changes or new requirements may constitute additional scope.

Can you work with raw footage?

Yes, provided the footage and brief are sufficiently organized.

Can you create multiple formats?

Additional adaptations must be confirmed during scope discussion and may carry additional charges.

Are project or source files included?

Not by default. They can be discussed separately.

Can you do rush projects?

Subject to availability and potentially additional charges.

Do you handle long-form YouTube?

Yes. It's one of our five core service pillars.

Do you provide pre-production and content strategy?

Yes, depending on the engagement. Both are stages in our process.

What happens if the project is outside the package?

Mufime will review the requirements and recommend the appropriate scope before production.
